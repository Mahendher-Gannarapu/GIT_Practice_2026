Explain Test Strategy in detail?
A Test Strategy is a high-level document that defines the general approach and principles of testing within the organization or project. 
It is typically created by test managers and is used across multiple projects.
It includes:
•	Testing objectives and quality targets
•	Types of testing to be performed
•	Test levels (unit, integration, system, UAT)
•	Testing tools and standards
•	Test design techniques
•	Roles and responsibilities
•	Configuration and defect management approach
•	Test metrics and reporting
